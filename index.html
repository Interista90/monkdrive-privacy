MonkDrive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MonkDrive app for Android ("MonkDrive", "the app") handles your information. MonkDrive is developed and published by KYO Tech ("we", "us").

In short:

  • MonkDrive works entirely on your phone. The app has no internet access and does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your exact location is used only while detecting and measuring your drives. It is never stored in your trip history or shared.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share it, for example a trip image or an email to our support.

1. Who is responsible

KYO Tech is the developer of MonkDrive and is responsible for this policy. You can contact us about privacy at monkdrivesafe@gmail.com.

2. What MonkDrive accesses and why

MonkDrive helps you stay focused while driving. To do this, it uses the following information on your device, and only for the purposes described:

  • Location (precise and approximate, including in the background). Used to detect when a drive starts and ends and to measure speed and distance. If you allow location "all the time", MonkDrive can also detect drives when the app is closed or after your phone restarts. Location is processed on your device; trip summaries contain distance, time and score, but no coordinates or routes.
  • Physical activity. Android's activity recognition (provided by Google Play services) tells the app whether you are likely in a vehicle, so that drive detection uses less battery.
  • Bluetooth and car connection. Used to recognise when your phone connects to your car, which helps detect the start of a drive.
  • Accessibility service. Used only to read the name of the app that is currently open, so MonkDrive can show its focus screen over distracting apps during a drive. MonkDrive does not read your screen content, the text you type or your messages, and does not perform actions on your behalf.
  • Notifications. While a drive is in progress, MonkDrive hides notifications from other apps to reduce distraction. It uses only the name of the app that posted a notification and never reads the notification's content.
  • Do Not Disturb. MonkDrive can switch Do Not Disturb on during a drive and off again when the drive ends.
  • Display over other apps. Used to show the focus screen during a drive.

You can refuse or later revoke any of these permissions in Android Settings. Some features will then not work.

3. What MonkDrive stores on your device

  • Trip history and scores: start and end time, distance, Zen score, number of hidden distractions and similar trip statistics.
  • Profile details you enter: an optional name and email address. They are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.
  • App settings, such as your paired car's label and Passenger Mode.
  • Diagnostic logs: technical event and error records used to keep the app reliable. They may include the names of apps opened while the focus screen is active. In the version of the app you download from Google Play, these logs do not contain location coordinates.

All of this information stays on your phone. MonkDrive has no account system and no server, and we cannot access your data.

4. When information leaves your device

  • When you share. If you tap a share button, MonkDrive creates an image (for example your trip score) and passes it to the app you choose. What happens next is governed by that app.
  • When you contact support. The Help screen opens your own email app with a message to monkdrivesafe@gmail.com. We receive only what you decide to send and use it only to answer you.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up MonkDrive's data (such as trip history, profile details and settings) to your Google account. That backup is controlled by you and handled by Google. Diagnostic logs are excluded from backup.
  • Google Play services. Location and activity recognition are provided through Google Play services on your phone, which Google operates under its own Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers or use it for any purpose other than the app's features.

5. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data stays on your device until you delete it. To delete all MonkDrive data, open Android Settings › Apps › MonkDrive › Storage and tap "Clear storage", or uninstall the app. To delete a backup, manage your backups in your Google account. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request; you can ask us to delete them at any time.

6. Security

Your data is kept in MonkDrive's storage on your device and is protected by Android's app security model. Because the app has no internet access, your data is never transmitted over the network by MonkDrive.

7. Children

MonkDrive is intended for drivers and is not directed to children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because MonkDrive keeps your data only on your device, you can view and delete it yourself at any time. For any question or request, email monkdrivesafe@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how MonkDrive handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
